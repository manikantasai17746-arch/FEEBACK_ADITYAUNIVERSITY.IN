What happens after you submit

  1. 1

    Students answer

    Every student gets a form for each subject and faculty member, and answers it without a name attached.

  2. 2

    Results are compiled

    Ratings and comments are grouped by subject, faculty member and department.

  3. 3

    Faculty see published results

    Results reach faculty only after the form is published, so nothing arrives half-counted.

  4. 4

    Departments follow up

    HODs and the Dean review results across departments and decide what needs attention.

Your answers are stored apart from your name

Three rules the system follows, so that feedback can stay honest.

Submitted is tracked, answers are not
The system notes that you finished a form so you are not asked twice. That record is kept separately from what you answered.
Comments can’t be traced by order
Comments are not listed in the order they were submitted, so their position never points back to a person.
Nothing is shared early
Faculty see results only after the form is published.
